# Little Little Flower Alarm 🌻

**[Visit the website →](https://xuwupeng2000.github.io/sunflower/en/)** · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823854490)

For people who plan life by the day of the week.

If you plan your life by the day of the week, Little Little Flower Alarm is for you. Work on Monday, school pickup on Wednesday, a class on Friday, a slower start at the weekend: choose what happens on each weekday and when you want a reminder. Your plans repeat every week. Need a one-off change? Skip it once; next week stays on track. See weekly routines and one-time plans together in Schedule.

**No ads. No subscriptions. No internet required. 100% yours.** Your alarms, reminders, and flower progress stay on your device.

## Make room for your week

- **Weekly alarms:** set different reminder times for Monday through Sunday. Skip the next occurrence or pause a weekday when plans change.
- **Schedule:** see weekly routines and one-time reminders together, organized into this week, next week, and the weeks ahead. Set an arrival time and a reminder to leave.
- **Timer:** save named countdowns for workouts, tea, cooking, or a break. Reuse your favorite durations without setting them up again.
- **A flower to grow with you:** earn fertilizer by snoozing alarms, creating reminders, and starting timers. Choose a sunflower, moth orchid, clivia, cyclamen, or kalanchoe and a planter of your own. Water it, tap it to see it sway, and use fertilizer to grow your sunflower taller.

Built for iPhone with iOS 26 system alarms, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island controls. Timers use notifications and Live Activities.

Available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Italian, Spanish, and Russian.

**[Explore screenshots, the comic, feature details, and FAQs on GitHub Pages →](https://xuwupeng2000.github.io/sunflower/en/)** The website is available in all ten languages.

## Development

Made with Godot, with a native iOS AlarmKit integration. Flowers and pots are Blender `.glb` assets in `assets/`; scenes live in `scenes/`, styling in `theme/ui.tres`, and translations in `locale/ui.csv`.

Run the existing test suite from the repository root:

```sh
python3 tests/check.py
```

The test runner requires Godot at `/Applications/Godot.app` and the configured iPhone connected and available. It uses a temporary project named **Sunflower Alarm Test**, backs up and restores device and desktop alarm data, and writes its report to `test-report/index.html`.
