Privacy Policy

Little Little Flower Alarm · Effective date: 2026-10-17

The short version: this app doesn't collect, upload or share any of your personal data. Your alarms and your flower stay on your iPhone 🌱

What we don't collect

  • The app makes no network requests and has no server.
  • There are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • No ads, no analytics, no cross-app tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your iPhone:

  • your alarms, reminders, paused and skipped days, and timer presets;
  • your flower's growth, watering and fertilizer progress, and your chosen flower and pot;
  • your language preference.

This data lives in the app's own storage. Some of it is placed in an on-device App Group container so the app's widget and Live Activities can read it. It never leaves your device and we can't see it.

System permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): to ring at the times you set.
  • Notifications: for things like a finished timer.

These are used only to schedule alarms and reminders on your device. You can turn them off anytime in iPhone Settings.

Information from Apple

If you choose to share with app developers in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off at any time.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all the data it stored on your device. Since we have no servers, there's nothing else to delete.

Children's privacy

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
